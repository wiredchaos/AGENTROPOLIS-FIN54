# Sample Macro Risk Report

## Inflation

- CPI remains above its trailing average and is still rising on a three-month view.
- Core CPI is cooling gradually but remains elevated versus long-run comfort levels.

## Labor Market

- Unemployment is stable near cycle lows, indicating limited labor slack.

## Rates Yield Curve

- Policy rates remain restrictive while the 10Y-2Y spread is inverted.

## Credit Stress

- Credit spreads are contained but no longer at cycle tights.

## Recession Indicators

- Recession probability gauges remain low but are drifting upward from trough readings.
